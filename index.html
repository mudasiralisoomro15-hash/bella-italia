Our Story

Three generations of Italian heritage

What began as Nonna Rosa's small kitchen in Florence has grown into one of New York's most beloved Italian dining destinations. Yet our soul remains unchanged — handmade pasta rolled each morning, sauces simmered for hours, and ingredients sourced from the finest Italian producers.

Every dish at Bella Italia is a celebration of family, of patience, and of the simple belief that great food brings people together.

— Marco Rossi
Executive Chef & Owner
